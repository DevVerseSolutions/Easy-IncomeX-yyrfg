Frequently Asked Questions
How are service rewards calculated?
Each valid submission is reviewed by our operations team. Once verified, the exact reward configured by the system is credited to your balance.
What are the withdrawal fees?
Withdrawal processing includes an administrator-configured fee (typically 0% - 5%) to cover payment gateway costs. The net calculation is clearly displayed prior to submitting.
When will my payout arrive?
Withdrawals are processed manually via bKash, Nagad, or Binance Pay within 1 to 24 hours.
